From first message to certificate

Five steps. You can start at any of them.

  1. Choose or request a course

    Pick from the catalogue, or tell us what you need and we will build a class around it.

  2. Pick how you learn

    Physical class by timetable (at our venue or yours), live online, or recorded lessons you watch whenever you like.

  3. Register and pay

    Create your free account. Pay by M-PESA STK Push to unlock a course. Free courses open straight away.

  4. Learn and practise

    Follow videos, do guided practicals and finish a mini project. Your dashboard tracks progress.

  5. Get certified

    Complete every lesson and download your Geopram Academy certificate.

    Payments, email and verification that work the first time

    Most platforms stall on the same three things: getting paid, sending reliable email and proving who someone is. We set them up properly, then test every failure path.

    M-PESA STK Push

    The payment prompt appears on your customer's phone straight from your checkout. We handle the callback and mark the order paid, cancelled or failed.

    Payment reconciliation

    Receipts matched to orders and customers, with duplicates and failed attempts flagged for you.

    Gmail API setup

    OAuth2 connection, verified sender and branded templates, so receipts, invoices and alerts leave from your own address and land in the inbox.

    OTP verification

    One-time codes with expiry, retry limits and secure storage, for sign-up, password reset and sensitive actions.

    Drive and Sheets sync

    Automatic backups and live reports in the tools your team already opens every morning.

    And more: webhooks, B2C and C2B flows, role-based access and connections to the systems you already use.
